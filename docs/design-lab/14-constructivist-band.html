14 · Diagonal Plate — constructivism as an interface

Provenance — El Lissitzky, Beat the Whites with the Red Wedge (1919, van Abbemuseum), for the wedge, and MoMA acc. 125.2015, Ivan Picelj's silkscreen after Lissitzky (1969), for the diagonal plate and the overprint. A poster is photographed, never spec'd: there is no published token file for constructivism, so this palette is DERIVED — paper #f4efe6, ink #111111, red #d8232a, deep red #8f1216, screen blue #1c3f94, read off the two artefacts' ink density. The dark half is an INVENTED EDITION: no constructivist poster was printed on a black ground, so field #141312, ink #f4efe6 and both lifted inks (#f0464c, #6f9bee) are authored here and labelled as authored. Licence question does not arise for the graphics; the type is OFL 1.1, verified at google/fonts/main/ofl/bigshouldersdisplay/OFL.txt, ofl/sairastencilone/OFL.txt and ofl/overpass/OFL.txt. Computed in this file, not quoted: red on paper is 4.37:1, so it FAILS 4.5:1 as 13px text and appears only as area, rule or plate; the lifted red on the dark field is 5.02:1; blue on paper 8.39:1; ink on paper 16.5:1. The one place the wedge has to carry a 13px row is the selection, and paper-white on it is 4.37 — so --primary-foreground is stock white, which measures 5.01:1 on the same red. That is the single token this direction had to invent to stay legal, and it is the finding worth keeping: a poster red is fine as an area and fine as a knockout to pure white, and is not fine to the paper it was printed on.

Type plan — Big Shoulders Display (condensed industrial, variable 100–900) carries every label and every display line: section headers 40/40 uppercase 700, the banner headline 96/96, and the mode pill, the tab/section/table heads and the agent pills at 12–13/16 caps with .1em tracking — tracking is what buys back the legibility the condensed width costs, and it is the one call here a reviewer should judge live. Saira Stencil One appears once, on the banner's RED WEDGE at 24px. Overpass is everything anyone actually reads: rows 13/17, prose 17/26, and paths, metrics and tool verbs 12/16 in Overpass Mono — numerals stay mono by design (mock 17's alignment mechanics), never the condensed display face. The reason the stencil is a single word is arithmetic: slab and stencil forms close their counters below about 20px, so at 13px a stencil colors.css is unreadable — a stencil face is a display face, and any direction that says otherwise is selling a texture. Misregistration (--misreg: 2px red, −2px blue) is legal only on the display word, where the copies land outside the counters; at body size it is a doubling artefact.

Geometry — 4px grid, --r/--r-md/--r-sm/--r-xs all 0, and no blur anywhere: --elev is a hard 4px 4px 0 0 offset ink plate. Rules are plates — 3px --rule-ink on ::before/::after, not 1px borders — drawn on the head, the section labels, the footer, every table row and the selection indicator (a 3px black plate sitting in the row's 4px left gutter). The rotated band is -8deg and lives inside a wrapper with overflow: clip, deliberately not hidden, so .row.foc's inset ring survives. The Electron hazard this direction actually carries: mix-blend-mode composites against whatever is behind the element in its stacking context, and in a transparent: true frameless window that backdrop is the user's desktop — so a blended layer without an opaque ancestor paints a black box (and every Radix portal lands in document.body, outside the ancestor you thought you gave it). Hence isolation: isolate plus background-color on .head, .gu .h, .desktop and .col, and hence --band-blend is a token: multiply where the ground is paper, screen in the dark edition, because overprinting a near-black ground prints nothing.

  • Move 1Rewrite src/renderer/styles/tokens/colors.css as a five-ink ramp (paper, ink, red, deep red, screen blue) and re-point the pairs in semantic/theme.css; --elev/--elev-sm/--key in src/renderer/styles/tokens/shadow.css become hard offset plates with no blur. Add --band-blend, --misreg, --halftone and --overprint to the theme blocks, never to :root — a :root value that references a theme-scoped token is invalid at computed-value time and vanishes in every descendant (mock 07's mesh died exactly this way).
  • Move 2src/renderer/styles/tokens/radius.css to 0 across every rung including the pristine ui/ primitives, and src/renderer/styles/tokens/typography.css to three stacks (--display, --stencil, Overpass) with the new 13/17 and 17/26 rungs — the two sizes the palette and the answer actually run at.
  • Move 3The devices cannot be tokens. The band, the halftone field and the selection plate need elements or pseudo-elements, so they go in the two component-adjacent stylesheets that already hold component classes: src/renderer/styles/globals.css (palette header, rows, footer) and src/renderer/styles/ask-mode-perplexity.css (answer opener, generative-UI card). Both must also carry the isolation: isolate + opaque-ancestor pair per blended surface.

Cost: markup, not tokens. Blends need isolation plus an opaque plate on every blended surface and a stacking review against tokens/z-index.css, because portals escape the ancestor; the halftone and the rotated band need pseudo-elements the current components do not render; the answer opener is a ::first-line trick that only holds while the first sentence is short; and any bundled font has to be vendored through the Vite asset pipeline — electron.vite.config.ts copies only sql-wasm.wasm and Automation.node, so non-code runtime resources are not packaged by default and a .woff2 read from disk would silently vanish in a production build.

Biggest risk: rotation destroys the vertical scanning rhythm a 30-row list depends on — a -8deg rule across an 800px row displaces its ends by ~56px, so the angle stays on chrome (banner, header, brief) and never touches row text, section labels or table rules. Second: a 4px-pitch halftone moirés against anti-aliased 13px glyphs, which is why the field sits at 8% on the desk plate and the column backdrop, behind the opaque card, and never under a row. Third: the red that carries this direction is 4.37:1 and cannot be a 13px label in either theme, so emphasis has to come from plate area and cap tracking, not from hue.
