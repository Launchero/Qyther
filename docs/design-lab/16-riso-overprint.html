16 · Two-ink print, misregistered — a risograph duplicator laying two fluoro plates + black

Provenance — ink values from the Martian Press riso ink chart (hexes given with Pantone, and the chart labels them "approximate"): Fluorescent Pink #FF48B0 (806 U), Scarlet #F65058 (185 U), Orange #FF6C2F (021 U), Sunflower #FFB511 (116 U), Yellow #F7FF00 (803 U), Kelly Green #67B346 (368 U), Blue #0078BF (3005 U), Cornflower #62A8E5 (292 U), Violet #9D7AD2 (265 U), Midnight #435060 (296 U), Black #000000, Light Gray #88898A (424 U). The honest evidence is that there is no canonical screen value for these inks. BYU's Print Lab lists Fluorescent Pink as #F75295 where Martian Press says #FF48B0 — a 145°-hue / big-saturation disagreement between two published charts for the same physical drum. That gap is printed into the banner, because a design system built on riso must not pretend its colours are precise. The paper field #f6f1e7 is DERIVED (a warm cream; not a published value). Exactly two process inks plus black run on each screen — Fluorescent Pink + Blue, with Midnight as the body ink; a third plate is the error state.

Type plan — --ff is Inter or Overpass (both verified OFL, embeddable) for UI; --mono is JetBrains Mono (already the app's mono) for paths, meta and numerals — on a machine without them both fall back to system-ui / Cascadia Mono. The specimen face --disp is Space Grotesk — the on-trend grotesque for this look — but its licence was UNVERIFIED this session, so it is display-only and cannot be shipped as-is (it silently falls back to Inter here). Rows set 13/17, meta 12/16 in mono, the header runs 32 caps, and the hit-count numeral is 40px — riso pairs a neutral grotesque with oversized numerals.

Geometry — 4px grid, radius 0 or 2px only, border: 1px solid rgba(0,0,0,.55) as the keyline. There is no shadow. Depth is a second plate: an offset of 2px in the second ink at opacity:.85; mix-blend-mode:multiply — that offset is the misregistration. Texture is pure CSS: halftone dots radial-gradient(currentColor 1px, transparent 1.5px) at background-size: 3px 3px rotated 15° on a pseudo-element; paper grain as two crossed repeating-linear-gradients at 2.5% black; overprint as two stacked multiply layers.

Contrast — computed and binding: Blue #0078BF on paper is ~4.1:1 → fails 4.5, so Blue is a fill or 16px+ only (never 13px body); Midnight #435060 on paper is ~8.4:1 — that is the body ink; Yellow #F7FF00 on paper is ~1.1:1 → never text, it is only ever a plate with a dark ink on it (Yellow under black ≈ 14:1). The dark half is a full re-derivation — an ink-dyed sheet with the fluoro plates kept bright — because a cream field cannot be the app's --background as-is.

Electron hazard, handled: mix-blend-mode: multiply inside a transparent, frameless window composites against the window's transparent backdrop, so an un-ancestored blend layer paints a black box. Every blend/halftone layer here lives inside an isolation: isolate ancestor that carries an opaque --background/--card (the `.page`, `.desktop`, `.palette`, `.hero`, and `.row.sel`); adopt the same rule — a multiply plate may never be a direct child of the transparent root.

  • Move 1Colour + the plate language: src/renderer/styles/tokens/colors.css gains the riso ink ramp (each with a .dark half — a hue primitive may never be text in this repo) and radius.css drops to 0/2. Depth moves to shadow.css: delete the blurred scale, add one non-blurred offset-plate token (2px 2px 0 0 <second-ink>) — that token is the misregistration.
  • Move 2Texture lives in the two component-adjacent stylesheets only — every mix-blend-mode plate, the halftone ::after, and the crossed repeating-linear-gradient paper grain go in src/renderer/styles/globals.css or ask-mode-perplexity.css, never inline and never a new stylesheet; each blend element needs an opaque ancestor because of the transparent-window hazard.
  • Move 3Signature markup needs three edits: ModeTabs.tsx becomes the two overlapping multiply plates (Files + Ask), ResultsList.tsx carries the pink misregistration plate behind the selected row, and CommandPaletteFooter.tsx gains the printer's colour bar (six 8px swatches flush right). The oversized hit-count numeral wraps its digits in the yellow block at ResultsList.tsx / TaskMetrics.tsx.

Cost — mostly tokens plus two CSS classes: tokens/colors.css gains the ink ramp with .dark halves, radius.css moves to 0/2, shadow.css swaps its blurred scale for one non-blurred offset-plate token, and typography.css adds 13/17/32/40 rungs; every blend/halftone layer lives in globals.css or ask-mode-perplexity.css, plus the three signature-markup edits above.

Biggest risk — mid-lightness acid inks are contrast traps at 13px (Blue ~4.1:1, Yellow ~1.1:1 on the cream — the numbers above); a heavy halftone dot field moirés against anti-aliased text and reads as noise, not texture; and a cream paper field cannot become --background without authoring a real .dark half or it collapses the app's measured dark surface ramp (background < sidebar < card < muted < popover).
