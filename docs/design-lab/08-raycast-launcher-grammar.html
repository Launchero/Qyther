08 · Launcher Grammar — Raycast's theme system with a Catppuccin skin

Provenance — Raycast's theme schema is exactly twelve slots plus an appearance flag: background, backgroundSecondary, text, selection, loader, red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, magenta. The registry raycast/ray-so is MIT, so the two published themes quoted here are redistributable verbatim: the dark half is Catppuccin Mocha (#1E1E2E / #1E1E2E / #CDD6F4 / #6C7086 / #7F849C, then #F38BA8 #FAB387 #F9E2AF #A6E3A1 #89B4FA #B4BEFE #CBA6F7) and the light half is Catppuccin Latte (#EFF1F5 / #EFF1F5 / #4C4F69 / #9CA0B0 / #8C8FA1, then #D20F39 #FE640B #DF8E1D #40A02B #1E66F5 #7287FD #8839EF); Catppuccin's palette is MIT too. The mapping onto our roles is background→--background and --card (same value — the card is not a surface), text→every -foreground, selection→--primary, loader→--loader (new role), green/yellow/blue/red→--success/--warning/--info/--destructive, purple→--agent, and magenta/blue/orange→--file-b/--file-a/--file-c. Raycast has no named greys: the only neutral ramp is its own lib/alpha.ts, 5% steps — 5→0D, 10→1A, 20→33, 25→40, 50→80, 75→BF, 90→E6 — and every hairline, chip ground and tint below is text or background plus exactly one of those steps, including --border (10), --mark-bg (20), the four -soft tints (10), and the kbd/.chip/.badge backgrounds (5 and 10). Even --plate is composed this way, though Raycast has no plate role at all.

Type plan — Inter (SIL OFL 1.1) for UI, JetBrains Mono (OFL) for paths and key caps; both declared here as stacks with system fallbacks and no link to any font host, and both would be self-hosted beside the app rather than fetched. Raycast's own HTML replica ships Inter, so this is the replica's type, not our invention. Scale stays 12/14/16/18/20/24 px; section labels move to 12/500 with .1px tracking, row titles to 14/500.

Geometry — the 56/40/40 rhythm measured from that replica: header 56 px, result row 40 px with a 12 px column gap and 8 px inline padding, row radius 10 px, section labels 12 px/500, footer bar 40 px, key caps 22×20 at radius 6 on text-at-5 (the ramp has no 6% step, so 5 is the nearest legal value). The card is borderless: --elev, --elev-sm and --key are all none, and only the header's bottom rule and the footer's top rule exist. Selection is a solid full-bleed selection pill with no shadow; the loader is a 2 px --loader hairline under the header, static in a mock that cannot animate a search. Two measured consequences of having no greys: the ramp step that clears 3:1 for a control boundary is 50 in Mocha and 75 in Latte — same slot, different step — and text-on-selection is 3.4:1 (Mocha) / 3.1:1 (Latte), large-text AA only. Motion is ours: 100–200 ms on cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1); no source publishes Raycast's curves.

  • Move 1Map the twelve slots onto our semantic roles and rewrite tokens/colors.css as an alpha-composited hairline system: two hexes per theme plus the lib/alpha.ts steps, deleting every named grey and every literal OKLCH neutral in semantic/theme.css.
  • Move 2Rhythm and corner language: tokens/spacing.css takes the 56/40/40 header-row-footer set with a 12 px row gap and 8 px inline padding, and tokens/radius.css takes 10 px on rows, 6 px on chips and key caps, 12 px on the card.
  • Move 3semantic/theme.css collapses the five-step surface ramp to background + backgroundSecondary + selection, because Raycast has no elevated-surface role: --card, --popover and the shadow tokens stop being distinct surfaces and become alpha tints.

Cost: token-only for colour and geometry — the twelve slots, the ramp and the 56/40/40 rhythm land in tokens/*.css and semantic/theme.css with no component edits. Two real additions besides that: the 40 px footer bar with its key-cap row in CommandPaletteFooter.tsx, and the 2 px loader hairline in CommandPaletteHeader.tsx. In this file the hairline is the only markup change from the control — one <div class="prog2"></div> after each .head — plus eight appended idiom rules.

Biggest risk: twelve slots under-specify a streaming chat. There is no code-block role, no popover role and no task-metrics role, so Ask mode has to invent all three by compositing text over background, which is precisely the freehand grey-choosing the schema was designed to remove; contrast then drifts per author (the 3.1–3.4:1 selection numbers above are the schema's own, not a mistake we can fix in CSS). Second, the look is so specific — borderless card, solid grey pill, alpha hairlines — that the app stops reading as Qyther and reads as a Raycast skin.
