02 · Canted Spectrum — Stripe's language with a shippable face

Provenance — Stripe's visual language: the deep-navy text pair, the four cool papers, the dominant violet ramp, the 12 px cards on a 1 px tinted border with a wide low-alpha shadow, and the rotated feathered gradient plane, all read from their public CSS. Their face is Söhne from Klim Type Foundry: an app-embedding licence is a separate agreement from desktop use, so Söhne is licensed and is not shippable here. The substitution declared below is Inter Display over Inter, both SIL OFL 1.1 — this mock embeds no font file, no CDN and no @font-face; the stacks name the face and fall back to the system.

Type plan — Inter Display for headings, Inter for UI (both OFL 1.1, roughly 48 KB for a Latin variable subset), IBM Plex Mono (OFL) for paths and code, chosen because Stripe's own CSS carries a SourceCodePro stack and Plex is the shippable near-neighbour. Scale 13/15/18/26/44/64 px; display leading 1.05 with -0.02em tracking above 26 px; 64 px is the marketing rung and appears on none of these five surfaces. Their uppercase micro-labels are 11px/600/+0.08em, but this app's own floor is 12 px — a 40 px row's mono path or an 18 px count badge at 11 px is unreadable at 125 % Windows scaling — so the labels keep the +0.08em tracking and sit at 12 px (--t12).

Geometry — 4 px base with a 20/28/40 rhythm; radius 12 on cards and dialogs, 8 on rows, 4 on chips, full pill only on the progress bar and the switch. Elevation is a 1 px tinted border plus one wide low-alpha shadow (--elev: 0 16px 40px -12px over 20 % of the foreground in light, over black in dark) — never a grey drop. The signature is exactly one structural move: a canted gradient plane, two literal Stripe gradients rotated -7deg and feathered with --plane-mask, clipped to the palette header band and nowhere else. No text sits on it: in the band every text-bearing element (the mode pill, the query field, the key caps, the ghost) is an opaque plate, and the result rows, the transcript body and both tables sit on opaque plates, so the plane never reaches them even clipped.

  • Move 1Ramp: tokens/colors.css takes the navy and violet scales and the :root / .dark blocks in semantic/theme.css re-point every role onto them — the dark half stops being neutral grey and becomes the brand navy, so --background and --foreground swap to the same file.
  • Move 2Depth and corners: tokens/shadow.css replaces the grey drops with tinted wide low-alpha shadows (--elev = 0 16px 40px -12px) and tokens/radius.css rises to 12 on cards, 8 on rows, 4 on chips. Both move the pristine components/ui/ primitives, which pull radius and shadow from the tokens.
  • Move 3The plane needs markup: src/renderer/components/CommandPaletteHeader.tsx gains a wrapper element carrying the rotated, masked gradient layer, because ::before content is not reachable from a Radix-themed class and the band must keep overflow: hidden and a stacking context; tokens/typography.css gains Inter Display and --ff-display above the existing --font-sans.

Cost: colour, radius and shadow are token-only — tokens/colors.css, tokens/shadow.css and tokens/radius.css, plus the role assignments in semantic/theme.css, with no component edit. The canted plane is not: it is wrapper markup in CommandPaletteHeader.tsx, a stacking context, a clip and a mask, and it must be re-derived for the tray-quoted and expanded heights. Motion is nearly free: the accordion ease and the durations land in tokens/motion.css.

Biggest risk: rotated gradient layers plus an alpha mask are exactly what paints worst in a transparent, always-on-top Electron window — the palette re-composites on the height tween and on every show/hide, and Chromium rasterises the rotated, masked plane rather than caching it. The second risk is contrast, not colour: #635bff on white measures 4.70:1, which is fine for 13 px body text and not for an 11 px label, and Stripe's bright status hues (the green, the orange and the red of the accent ramp) all fail as light-half text — the light block therefore declares darkened variants for --success, --warning, --info, --destructive and --muted-foreground, and keeps the bright hues only as fills.
