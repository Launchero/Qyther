22 · Cyanotype Plate — the engineering drawing: line weight is meaning, and the sheet has a filing margin

Provenance — the direction is a real 1842 artefact, not a vibe: John Herschel's cyanotype process was invented to copy **mathematical tables**, and Anna Atkins' British Algæ is the book made with it, whose impressions the V&A and the NYPL hold. The graphic law comes from three ISO standards, quoted from their published scope summaries because the texts themselves are paywalled (spec-derived, not package-derived): ISO 128 designates the line classes — A continuous wide, B continuous narrow, C dashed, D chain, E zig-zag — with nominal widths 0.13 / 0.18 / 0.25 / 0.35 / 0.5 / 0.7 / 1.0 / 1.4 mm, "line thickness should be not less than 0.25 mm", and border lines as continuous wide at minimum 0.7 mm; ISO 5457 sets the sheet: "the border shall be 20 mm wide on the left edge, including the frame. It can be used as a filing margin. All other borders are 10 mm wide"; ISO 3098 gives lettering heights as a √2 series on paper, 2.5 to 20 mm. Prussian blue #003153 is the printed value of pigment PB27, quoted from the Wikipedia article on the pigment; #EAF2F6 line white, #9FD0E8 annotation cyan, #D7572F tick vermillion and #06203A deep plate are DERIVED, and so is every mm→px mapping: this file is not paper. The light half is the reverse sheet — a white field with #003153 lines, which is what a modern CAD plot actually looks like — not a tint of the dark half.

Type plan — title block cells, dimension numerals and zone letters are uppercase --caps (JetBrains Mono, OFL 1.1, already shipped as --font-mono here) at 12 px with letter-spacing: .12em; plate titles are Jost (SIL OFL 1.1, verified) at 20 / 17, geometric, the face that reads as a drawing sheet; rows stay 13. Display specimen at 68. The size series is √2-stepped — 12 · 17 · 24 · 34 · 48 · 68 — the same ratio as ISO A-series paper, and the mapping to px is DERIVED because ISO 3098's rungs are millimetres. There is no 11px rung: the √2 series from 12 lands on 17, and every 11px the control ships (chips, metric labels, the caveat line) is lifted to 12, one of them by the only !important in the file because it sits in an inline style. Without Jost: Futura on macOS, Century Gothic on Windows with Office, then Segoe UI — a geometric sans either way, so the sheet still reads as a sheet. Mono without JetBrains Mono falls to Cascadia Mono / Consolas.

Geometry — strict 8 px module (--mod), radius 0, box-shadow: none everywhere: separation is done by the A/B weight pair and the margins, never by elevation, because a drawing has no material. --wA 2px = class A = the neatline, frame lines, visible outlines and the selection's extent lines; --wB 1px = class B = dimensions, leaders, hatching, table rules and zoning hairlines; class C dashed = provisional things (export chips, the pending step, the tooltip's leader); class D chain = centre lines and the step rail. One uniform hairline over navy is a tint, and that sentence is the whole direction. The 20 mm / 10 mm margin law is a fat left rail (--rail 24px) carrying hairline zoning ticks against a 12px content padding — the load-bearing fact is the 2 : 1 ratio, not a mm conversion. Palette stays 800 px wide, header 72, row floor 40; the selected row calls its own 40 out as a dimension. The only surviving shadow is .row.foc's inset ring — no offset, no blur, so it is a line.

  • Move 1Add --plate, --plate-ink, --plate-line-wide, --plate-line-narrow, --plate-annot, --plate-tick and the hatch / chain / rail-tick gradients to src/renderer/styles/tokens/colors.css, with both halves in semantic/theme.css — and keep the gradients inside the :root / .dark blocks, never above them, since they interpolate --plate-line-narrow at computed-value time. tokens/radius.css sets every rung to 0 and tokens/shadow.css sets --shadow-popover, --shadow-dialog, --shadow-tooltip, --shadow-toast, --shadow-shortcut-key and --shadow-inset to none: Radix popovers arrive with --shadow-popover and 8px corners, so they must be fixed through the tokens, not per component, or the pristine components/ui/ layer diverges from the plate.
  • Move 2tokens/typography.css: vendor Jost (OFL — a sandboxed renderer has no CDN), add --font-display and the √2 rungs, and delete the three 11px aliases --text-2xs / --text-xs-custom / --text-11px, which exist today only so an old var() cannot drop; grep them first, then promote the survivors to --text-2xs's 12px floor. Mono labels gain --tracking-plate: .12em.
  • Move 3The devices need markup, in one shared owner so it is authored once: a <span> leader inside each .sec section header (ResultsList.tsx), the title-block grid in ask-mode/Transparency/TaskMetrics.tsx and CommandPaletteFooter.tsx, the chain step rail in ask-mode/ThinkingSteps.tsx, the dimensioned selection + extent callout in ResultItem.tsx, and the hatch progress well in ask-mode/BrowserProgressPanel.tsx. --input's 3:1 control-boundary floor still binds inside the plate, so SearchBar.tsx, SettingsDialog.tsx and MCPToolModal.tsx keep a class-A boundary even when the sheet is Prussian.

Cost: MEDIUM — six new tokens with both halves (--plate, --plate-line-wide, --plate-line-narrow, --plate-ink, plus annot/tick and the hatch/chain/rail gradients), radius to 0, the shadow tokens to none, a vendored OFL display face, the 11px aliases deleted, and the five markup sites above. The Radix popover warning is the real price: --shadow-popover and --radius-lg are what give a popover its lift, so the fix must land in the token layer once — patching components instead forks components/ui/ and breaks shadcn diff. One further honest cost: every value here is sRGB hex, not OKLCH, because Prussian's printed value is published as hex — adopting this direction means either a hex island in colors.css against the repo's OKLCH mandate, or converting PB27 and losing the quotable number.

Biggest risk: the plate is an opaque dark field, so the direction is dark-only unless the reverse sheet is authored properly — and the reverse sheet is the hard half, because #9FD0E8 on white measures 1.66:1 and has no text role there at all, which is why the light half's annotation ink is a derived #2A5C7E (7.2:1). Second: the annotations are information, not ornament — a zone band, a chain line and a title block each cost the card vertical space, and a plate that shows more chrome than it shows result rows fights the 40px row target and the 32px control floor; the devices below are therefore all pseudo-elements on structure the app already has, and none of them is allowed to grow a row. Third: #9FD0E8 on Prussian clears AA at 8.1:1 (6.1:1 on the raised popover), which makes it a label colour, never a body colour — body prose is #EAF2F6 at 11.9:1 — and by the same law vermillion #D7572F fails as text in both halves (3.39:1 on Prussian, 3.97:1 on white), so it is a tick, a clip-path arrowhead, a --ring and a mark underline, and its text duties go to derived rungs #F08A5F / #A83A18. A plate that lets cyan set a paragraph is a wallpaper.
